With the right support, CuffWay will be the smarter way to safer respiratory recovery.

**NeedNoMore** is a civic-tech platform that makes real-time food access visible for everyone who depends on it. Families use the app to share quick updates after each visit to a food pantry (a place that gives free groceries), so others can see which pantries are open, stocked, and nearby — down to the types of food available. Pantries confirm updates and use a simple portal to manage hours, inventory, and alerts. Cities and healthcare providers use a live dashboard with real-time insight to spot shortages, identify neighborhoods lacking nutritious options, and direct support where it’s needed most. All three NeedNoMore products launched last week, pantries are actively onboarding, first users are already searching for food, and two citywide pilots begin in January.

**Co-Founders: Svitlana & Yurii Koval | hello@neednomore.com | NeedNoMore.com**

**Sorlaste Technologies** is a clean-energy engineering company developing compact electromechanical power systems that convert electromagnetic energy into controlled mechanical motion and on-demand electrical output. Our flagship technology, E-MPE (Electromagnetic Mechanical Power Engine), is a patent-pending modular electromagnetic engine architecture designed to scale across configurations and fit a wide range of power and mobility platforms.

Our purpose is to expand access to reliable, efficient power by delivering a controllable generation solution that is compact, adaptable, and built for real-world operating demands. We aim to enable greater energy resilience and flexibility supporting industries where needs like stationary backup and distributed power, mobile/remote power, and range extending architectures for next-generation electric platforms are in demand.

Sorlaste’s goal is turn innovative electromagnetic conversion into practical systems that improve uptime, reduce constraints for designers and operators, and accelerate the transition to cleaner, more dependable and affordable energy solutions.

**Vertical’s** platform helps athletes and coaches prevent energy crashes by turning a simple meal scan into predictive nutrition guidance—forecasting energy dips, satiety duration, and performance/focus risk based on what was eaten and the athlete’s context (game, practice, etc). Vertical simplifies nutrition decision-making by translating ingredients into actionable recommendations (what to eat next, when, and why), while giving coaches a lightweight view of team nutrition consistency and risk flags without requiring manual food logging. Vertical is currently preparing an early alpha (Jan–Feb) and building toward a March team pilot with youth and high-school programs, with a waitlist and prototype experience already live to validate demand for proactive, performance-first nutrition intelligence.

**Founder: Solomon Jacobs | solomon@verticalco.io |** [**verticalco.io**](https://verticalco.io)

[Launchpad COLA](https://boydinnovationcenter.squarespace.com/launchpad-cola) is an intensive entrepreneur training program that helps founders transform scalable tech startup ideas into real businesses. The program equips first-time founders and early-stage tech companies with expert mentorship, proven curriculum, and access to Columbia's growing entrepreneurial network.

Created and delivered by [Launchpad GVL](https://launchpadgvl.com/), the program draws on nationally recognized training including [Founder University](https://founder.university/) from Launch VC, [RevTech Labs Foundation](https://www.revtechlabs.co/), and The Bunker Labs Veterans in Residence program.
