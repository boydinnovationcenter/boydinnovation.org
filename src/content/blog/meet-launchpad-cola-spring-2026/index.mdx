The Spring 2026 Launchpad cohort has kicked off strong with **20 tech startups** ready to take their companies to the next level. This group was selected from nearly 40 applicants, and what gave them their edge? They've identified a real problem and solution, they're building scalable tech-focused businesses, and most importantly – they're all in.

That means showing up to in-person sessions every Tuesday at 7 AM, completing assignments that push them deeper into their business model and product development, meeting with mentors who challenge their assumptions, staying accountable to their fellow founders, and having the grit to keep pushing through when things get tough.

[Launchpad COLA](https://boydinnovationcenter.squarespace.com/launchpad-cola) is an intensive 12-week entrepreneur training program that helps founders transform scalable tech startup ideas into real businesses. The program equips first-time founders and early-stage tech companies with expert mentorship, proven curriculum, and access to Columbia's growing entrepreneurial network.

Created and delivered by [Launchpad](https://launchpadtv.com/) at the Boyd Innovation Center, the program draws on nationally recognized training including [Founder University](https://founder.university/) from Launch VC, [RevTech Labs Foundation](https://www.revtechlabs.co/), and The Bunker Labs Veterans in Residence program.
