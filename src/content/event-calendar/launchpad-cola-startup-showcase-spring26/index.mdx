### **See what Columbia is building!**

### <span class="text-accent">**Join us for an exciting showcase of the BIC’s Launchpad COLA Spring 2026 Tech Incubator Cohort.** </span>

*Space is limited. Reserve your spot now!*

Launchpad COLA is a 12-week tech incubator program powered by Launchpad Tech Ventures, a Greenville SC company, built upon nationally recognized and proven training curriculum. It’s founder boot camp.

35 tech-focused startup companies applied, 20 were accepted to the Spring 2026 Cohort at the Boyd Innovation Center.

After 12 weeks of early mornings, mentorship, refining, and building – 20 startups and their founders are ready to show Columbia what they've created.

### **What to Expect:**

- Meet the Spring 2026 Cohort founders and their game-changing startups. Play investor and vote on your favorite to win a special award.
- Hear stage pitches from this cohort’s top startups!
- Connect with entrepreneurs, investors, community leaders, tech professionals, and the innovation community from Columbia and across South Carolina.
- Drinks and light bites on us.

Come be a part of the celebration and help us LAUNCH these founders into their next chapter!

***Tickets are free, but space is limited. Save your spot today!***
