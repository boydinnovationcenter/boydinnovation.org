### **Know Your Numbers: The Financial Framework Every Founder Needs**

<span class="text-accent">***Finance Deep Dive Workshop***</span>

A solid P&L projection is the lifeblood of a new venture, allowing you to raise money and instill confidence. While creating one may seem overly difficult, it doesn’t have to be. It’s all about using a solid methodology and some simple math.

Join Lanford Holloway for a hands-on workshop where you'll see a basic auto-renewing revenue model built from scratch – no accounting degree required. Learn the framework to track what matters, test your assumptions, and plan for growth.

Walk out with a financial projections template and the clarity to run your business with confidence, not guesswork.

This event is FREE for BIC members.
