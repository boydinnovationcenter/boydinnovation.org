<p class="large">**Join Us for October’s *Tech on Tap* featuring founder of** [**TerraStride**](http://terrastride.com/) **and** [**HuntStand**](https://www.huntstand.com/)**, Lanford Holloway.**</p>

Lanford will share insights from the complete startup journey – from rough concept sketches to private equity exit. This isn't a polished success story; it's the real, uneven path of building a company over a decade.

Join us for drinks, light bites, meaningful connection, and lessons from the field. Bring someone, meet someone, and learn something!

---

<p class="large">***Tech on Tap* is a monthly community gathering to connect with Columbia’s tech scene and learn from pros who have built, scaled, and exited successful companies.**</p>

Join us every third Thursday evening for expert insights on the challenges that can make or break your tech startup—from incorporation decisions to scaling strategies.

It's networking with purpose: connect with fellow founders & tech professionals while learning from entrepreneurs who've guided successful companies through these exact decisions.

#### **Tech on Tap is for you if:**

- You're building, growing, or scaling a tech company.
- You're interested in starting a tech business.
- You're a technology professional looking to connect with others in Columbia's tech scene.

#### **Event Details**

➡️Doors (and bar) open at 5 pm<br />➡️Session begins at 5:30 pm<br />➡️Q&A and networking from 6 – 6:30 pm
