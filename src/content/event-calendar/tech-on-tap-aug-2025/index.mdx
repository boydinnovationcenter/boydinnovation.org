<p class="large">**Introducing Tech on Tap 🍻**</p>

<p class="large">**A new monthly series where Columbia's tech founders gather to tackle the decisions that matter most.**</p>

Join us every third Thursday evening for expert insights on the challenges that can make or break your tech startup—from incorporation decisions to scaling strategies.

It's networking with purpose: connect with fellow founders while learning from professionals who've guided successful companies through these exact decisions.

#### **Who should attend:**

- Early-stage tech entrepreneurs validating their idea or building an MVP
- Established founders gaining traction, growing, or scaling their startups
- Technical professionals considering the entrepreneurial leap or looking to join a startup
