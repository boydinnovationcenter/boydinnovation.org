## **Tech on Tap July 2026**

---

<p class="large">***Tech on Tap* is a monthly community gathering to connect with Columbia’s tech scene and learn from pros who have built, scaled, and exited successful companies.**</p>

<p class="large">**Every third Thursday evening we bring in an expert to share insights on the challenges that can make or break your tech startup—from incorporation decisions to scaling strategies.**</p>

<p class="large">**It's networking with purpose: connect with fellow founders & tech professionals while learning from entrepreneurs who've guided successful companies through these exact decisions.**</p>

#### <span class="text-accent">**Tech on Tap is for you if:**</span>

- **You're building, growing, or scaling a tech company.**
- **You're interested in starting a tech business.**
- **You're a technology professional or student looking to connect with others in Columbia's tech scene.**

#### <span class="text-accent">**Event Details**</span>

<p class="large">**➡️ Doors (and bar) open at 5 pm<br />➡️ Session begins at 5:30 pm<br />➡️ Q&A and networking from 6 – 6:30 pm**</p>
