<p class="large">**Join Us for Tech on Tap:** **The Power of Social Capital with Harold Hughes**<br /><span class="text-accent">Thursday, November 20th, 5 pm - 6:30 pm</span></p>

November's Tech on Tap features serial entrepreneur and angel investor Harold Hughes. Harold will share practical insights on building and leveraging social capital – the networks, connections, and relationships that fuel both personal and professional growth.

Whether you're building a business, looking for your next opportunity, or seeking strategic partnerships, trusted relationships are the foundation. Harold will break down how to intentionally develop your network and turn connections into real business value.

**Don't miss our final Tech on Tap of 2025!** Come ready for actionable takeaways, meaningful conversations, and insights from someone who's built multiple successful ventures through strategic relationships.

**This is for you if:**

- You're building, growing, or scaling a tech company
- You're interested in starting a tech business
- You're a technology professional looking to connect with Columbia's tech scene
- You're actively job searching or exploring new opportunities
- You're a student wanting to build your professional network
- You're curious about how relationships drive business success

**What to Expect:**

- Drinks and light bites
- Expert insights on building valuable networks
- Time to make genuine connections
- Practical strategies you can implement immediately
