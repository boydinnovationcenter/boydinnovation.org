This bring-your-own-laptop, hands-on workshop covers SEO basics that actually matter, setting up Google Analytics and Search Console, submitting sitemaps, identifying your target keywords, optimizing title tags and header structure, plus live demos and troubleshooting. Perfect for small business owners, founders, marketers, and anyone who's ever thought "I feel like I'm doing SEO wrong."

No prior experience needed—you’ll walk through everything together, tools included (SEO Meta in One Click, Google Search Console, Google Analytics, MetaMonster). Bring your laptop, expect snacks, and walk away with tangible SEO improvements.
